A little note about the parser

For a webpage link, this app sends the URL to Jina Reader to retrieve readable page text, then looks for English or Hebrew ingredient and instruction headings. Public recipe pages and some public Instagram captions work; paywalls, blocked pages, unavailable captions, and unusual layouts may not. Videos and audio are not transcribed.

The reader service is operated by a third party and its availability, limits, and data handling are subject to its policies. Don't import private or password-protected links. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm the draft. Recipe text you paste is parsed locally. For best results, put each ingredient and step on its own line under headings such as Ingredients / Instructions or מצרכים / אופן ההכנה.

This is structured extraction, not translation or an AI model. For AI-based extraction, a developer can connect the RecipeAI.extract adapter to a secure backend. Never embed a private API key in this file.
